# Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you land on the **Dashboard**—your home base in TroupeIT.

## Trial and upgrade

If you are on a **free trial** or your owned companies are not on a paid plan, the **top bar** and dashboard may show how many trial days are left and a path to **upgrade** or **start a trial** (for example after you create a company). When the trial is running low, you might also see a short **upgrade** reminder card (you can dismiss it; it may come back after a while).

[Subscription plans](/docs/companies/subscription-plans) cover what happens when the trial ends.

## Layout (desktop vs mobile)

On **large screens**, the page is roughly two columns:

- **Left**: Your **profile** card, then **Companies**, then a compact **activity** feed.
- **Right**: **Events** (upcoming and prior, with a company filter), then **Your Acts**.

On **phones and small tablets**, **activity** moves below **Your Acts** so the main lists stay higher on the page.

## Profile card

- Your **avatar** opens the image editor when clicked.
- Your **name** and **username** link to your public profile.
- **Edit profile** appears when you hover the card (or use **Edit Profile** in the avatar menu in the top bar).

## Companies list

- **New** creates another company; **Manage** opens the full company list/management view.
- Each row links to that company’s **profile page**.
- If you are a **stage manager** or higher on that company, you get shortcuts: **Files** (that company’s library), **Acts** filtered to that company, **settings** (gear), and **Invite** for **producers**.
- **Owners** see subscription/trial badges (for example **TRIAL · Xd**, **PAYMENT DUE**) that link to billing when relevant.

## Events

The **events** panel lets you filter by **All companies** or a single company. You’ll see **upcoming** and **prior** events in separate sections, with pagination when there are many.

## Your Acts

Lists acts you own, with **Create act**, pagination, and links into each act.

## Activity

Shows recent actions across your memberships (company-scoped where it applies).

## Chat

Use the **chat** button (usually a speech-bubble control on the edge of the screen) to open **team chat**: channels tied to companies, events, and shows, plus **direct messages**. You need to be on a troupe (company) to see most channels.

## Other shortcuts

- **Take a Tour** (when you have companies): a short guided tour of the dashboard.
- **Create Event** and **Create Act** are also in the **top navigation** so you can start them from any page (Create Event sends you to the dashboard if you are not already there).
- **Your Files** (personal file library) is in the **avatar menu** and, on small screens, in the main nav—see [File management](/docs/file-management).
